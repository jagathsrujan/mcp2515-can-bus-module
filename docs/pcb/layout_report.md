- Y1-L1 centre distance **28.1 mm** (>=10); Y1 to SW pad (U3.5) 25.9 mm; Y1 to nearest CAN-pair item (D3/L2/R16/JP1/U2 CAN pads) **11.1 mm** (>=5). Nothing but GND under Y1/C15/C16 on bottom (bottom keep-out for signals).
- Oscillator track length (pad centres): OSC1 5.85, OSC2 1.95, OSC2_X 5.76 mm (plan <=5 mm each: OSC1/OSC2_X **exceed by ~0.8-0.9 mm**, no vias, short symmetric top-layer; crystal placement limited by U1 courtyard).
- CAN pair (rev. after Opus review): **D3 now sits in the gap between J3 and J4 at (62.0,25.7)**, J3 moved to pin1 y=30.75, J4 to 10.5. Routed lengths pin -> D3 pad (pad centres): J3 CANH 18.89 / CANL 18.89 mm (straight 5.3 / 11.6); J4 CANH 25.56 / CANL 25.56 (straight 16.5 / 9.7); mismatch 0.00 on each. D3->U2 via R16/R17: 20.19 / 20.44 mm. The "<=5 mm routed" target is met only for J3 CANH (~5.3 straight); **not achievable for the other three pins**: J3/J4 pins are on a 5.08 mm pitch, so L3/H4/L4 are 9.7-16.5 mm from any single D3 position. Routing order is J3/J4 -> D3 pads -> trunk -> JP1/R11-R14 -> R16/R17 (L2) -> U2; D3 GND pad has 2 dedicated GND vias at (64.8,25.7)/(66.4,25.7) plus 2 stitching vias nearby. 0.25 mm tracks on the top layer; 4 vias (two bottom jumpers, CANL via 58.8 mm column and CANH via 58.0 column) because J3/J4 pin order alternates H,L,H,L; short stubs carry detours/one meander each (right-hand detours) to equalise lengths.

## 5. Deviations / open items
1. Board 70x50 instead of 60x45: SMD courtyard margins, 12 test points, two 15 mm terminal blocks plus 5 mm header and 0.25-0.5 mm courtesy gaps could not be packed in 60x45.
2. CAN pair has **4 vias** (CANH and CANL bottom jumpers next to D3) and TX/RX/INT/MISO-type signals use a few bottom jumpers (STB, RXCAN, INT_U/INT_N, RST). Reason (CAN): J3 and J4 are identical outward terminals so pin order H,L,H,L along the edge is topologically non-planar on one layer; frozen schematic forbids swapping. Plan stated "no vias" - report.
3. OSC1/OSC2_X 5.8 mm (>5 mm target).
4. The CAN pair is not tightly coupled over its whole length (D3 pads/R16/R17/L2 geometry); skew is zero by construction.
5. Decoupling caps: centre-distance >2 mm for wide-pad ICs (see list); edge gaps <=2 mm.
6. Power tracks: some 0.3 mm instead of 0.5 mm; +5V and VIO routes are long diagonals (auto-routed) - functional, DRC clean, cosmetic.
7. No VIN_BUCK pour; Z3/Z4 are modest local pours.
8. J3-J5 use Phoenix 3D model (approximate); J1 has no 3D model (blank in renders).
9. Schematic footprint fields for J3/J4/J5 changed (non-electrical). docs BOM CSVs from phase 6 still show the old footprint name.
10. JLC order-number placeholder text and silk board name are present; legend of J2 pins uses two staggered rows above the header.

11. Rev. review fixes: D3 relocated to J3/J4 gap (see section 3); D2 edge clearance fixed; silk: "+" next to J5 pin 1 and "9V + centre" beside J1.
